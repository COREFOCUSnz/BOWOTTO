# Car model credit

`revuelto_hq_source.glb` is **"Lamborghini Revuelto"** (https://skfb.ly/pM6pA)
by **DRIVER-FIRE**, licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0**
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

`revuelto.glb` is the same model converted for the simulator: rotated so the
nose faces +Z, wheels re-parented to `wheel_fl/fr/rl/rr` pivots, textures
capped at 2k and re-encoded, vertex positions quantised to 16-bit and normals
to 8-bit (KHR_mesh_quantization). The geometry, textures and material set-up
are the author's.

The licence allows commercial use and derivatives provided the author is
credited. Keep this credit with any copy of the simulator that includes the
model.

Earlier builds used "Free Lamborghini Revuelto" by ALIEEEN (Sketchfab,
CC BY 4.0), since replaced.
