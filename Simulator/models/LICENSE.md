# Car model credit

`revuelto_hq_source.glb` is **"Free - High Quality Lamborghini Revuelto"**
from Sketchfab (author and licence line to be confirmed from the model page;
downloaded as a free model). `revuelto.glb` is the same model converted for
the simulator: rotated so the nose faces +Z, wheels re-parented to
`wheel_fl/fr/rl/rr` pivots, textures capped at 2k and re-encoded, vertex
positions quantised to 16-bit and normals to 8-bit (KHR_mesh_quantization).
The geometry, textures and material set-up are the author's.

Keep this credit with any copy of the simulator that includes the model.

Earlier builds used "Free Lamborghini Revuelto" by ALIEEEN (Sketchfab,
CC BY 4.0), which has since been replaced.
