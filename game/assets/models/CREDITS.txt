Character models
----------------
"All of the team Fortress 2 red team Mercenaries" by inonshalev42
Published on Sketchfab, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY).

The source model is a single 25 MB glTF binary containing eight characters
(Scout, Sniper, Soldier, Medic, Heavy, Pyro, Spy, Engineer) with no animation
clips. For this game they were:

  - split into one file per class
  - normalised to a common orientation, scale and floor level
  - reduced from 74-94 source bones to a shared 23-bone rig, with skin
    weights merged onto the nearest surviving ancestor bone
  - reduced to the source LOD 0 mesh per body part
  - quantised (int16 positions, int8 normals, uint16 UVs)
  - textures downscaled to at most 512 px and re-encoded as JPEG
  - animated procedurally at runtime (no clips exist in the source)

Rebuild with:  tools/build-assets.sh <source.glb>
